# Wayne Metro Community Action Agency

Wayne Metropolitan Community Action Agency (Wayne Metro) is a nonprofit community action agency that serves Wayne County and southeastern Michigan. We help people take on what gets in their way, like a shutoff notice, an eviction, a tax foreclosure, or a budget that doesn't add up, so they can work toward goals they set for themselves.

> Wayne Metro empowers individuals and communities to realize their own goals.

## What we do

We run more than 80 programs. They fall into five areas:

| Area | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Family** | Support for children, parents, and households |
| **Water & Energy** | Water bill assistance, energy assistance through MEAP, and home energy help |
| **Housing** | HUD housing programs and help staying housed |
| **Financial Wellness** | Free one-on-one financial counseling at the Financial Empowerment Center, and property tax help through the Detroit Tax Relief Fund and HOPE |
| **Community** | Programs that strengthen neighborhoods and connect residents to resources |

You can find the full list of programs at [waynemetro.org](https://www.waynemetro.org).

## About this GitHub

Our staff use this account for internal tools, data work, and automation that support our programs and make it easier for residents to get help. Most of our repositories are private because they handle program operations. We publish public repositories here when we can.
